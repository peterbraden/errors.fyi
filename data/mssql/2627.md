---
name: "Violation of PRIMARY KEY or UNIQUE Constraint"
description: "An INSERT or UPDATE attempted to create a duplicate value in a column protected by a PRIMARY KEY or UNIQUE constraint, and the statement was terminated. Find the conflicting row and either remove the duplicate, change the value being inserted, or use a MERGE / 'IF NOT EXISTS' pattern to avoid the collision."
