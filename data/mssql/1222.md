name: "Lock Request Time Out Period Exceeded"
description: "A statement was blocked waiting for a lock longer than the configured LOCK_TIMEOUT setting and was cancelled. This usually points to long-running transactions or a blocking chain; identify the head blocker (e.g. via sys.dm_exec_requests) and shorten the transactions holding locks."
