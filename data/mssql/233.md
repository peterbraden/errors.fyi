name: "No Process Is on the Other End of the Pipe"
description: "The connection was established but closed before the login handshake finished, typically surfaced as 'Shared Memory Provider: No process is on the other end of the pipe.'. This can occur when the instance is still starting up, when a forced-encryption setting mismatches between client and server, or when the server's connection limit has been reached."
