name: "Constraint Conflict"
description: "The INSERT, UPDATE, or DELETE statement conflicted with a constraint. A FOREIGN KEY, CHECK, or REFERENCE constraint blocked the operation because the data does not satisfy the constraint condition. Verify that referenced rows exist (for FK) or values are within the allowed range (for CHECK)."
