title: "ACME Protocol Error Codes"
description: "Standard error types defined by RFC 8555 (Automatic Certificate Management Environment), returned in the \"type\" field of a problem document as urn:ietf:params:acme:error:<code>. Used by Let's Encrypt and other ACME-compatible certificate authorities via clients such as Certbot, acme.sh, and win-acme."
