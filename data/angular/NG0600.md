name: "Writing to signals not allowed in computed or effect"
description: "A signal was written to inside a computed() or effect() function without using untracked(). Computed expressions and effects must be side-effect-free with respect to signal writes; use untracked() or move the write outside the reactive context."
references:
  - https://angular.dev/errors/NG0600
