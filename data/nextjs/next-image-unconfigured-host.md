---
name: "Next Image Unconfigured Host"
description: "The hostname of an external image URL has not been added to the remotePatterns list in next.config.js. Next.js requires you to explicitly allow external image domains for the next/image component to prevent abuse of the image optimization endpoint. Add the hostname under images.remotePatterns in your configuration."
references:
