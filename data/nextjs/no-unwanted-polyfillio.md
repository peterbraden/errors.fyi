---
name: "No Unwanted Polyfill.io"
description: "A script loading polyfills from polyfill.io is present in the application. Next.js already bundles the polyfills required by its browser support targets, making polyfill.io redundant and a potential performance and security risk (the service can inject arbitrary JavaScript). Remove the polyfill.io script tag."
references:
  - https://nextjs.org/docs/messages/no-unwanted-polyfillio
---
