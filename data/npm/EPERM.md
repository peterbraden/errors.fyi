name: "Operation Not Permitted"
description: "npm's underlying filesystem call (often rename, unlink, or mkdir) was rejected by the operating system, most commonly on Windows when a file is locked by another process such as an editor, antivirus scanner, or running dev server. Close programs that may be holding a lock on the path, retry, or run as Administrator/root as a last resort."
